HawkEye Stealer Infection High severity

SOC incident analysis of a network capture · 10 April 2019 · CyberDefenders HawkEye lab

Summary

A workstation (BEIJING-5CD1-PC, 10.4.10.132) downloaded and ran a HawkEye Keylogger – Reborn v9 payload, then emailed the user's saved browser and Outlook credentials to an attacker-controlled mailbox. The capture shows the download, the malware's check-ins and the data theft. It does not show the original phishing email.

Read the full report (PDF) Detection rules

What happened

  1. +48 s – Host downloads tkraw_Protected99.exe (about 2 MB) from proforma-invoices.com over plain HTTP.
  2. +68 s – Malware looks up its public IP at bot.whatismyipaddress.com, then repeats every ~10 minutes.
  3. +69 s – Malware logs in to a mailbox on macwinlogistics.in (SMTP port 587) and sends a password dump.
  4. Every ~10 min – The same email is sent again (7 sessions in 64 minutes).
